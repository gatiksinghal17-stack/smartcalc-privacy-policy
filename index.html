SmartCalc

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

SmartCalc ("the App") is a calculator and educational application that provides mathematical calculations, educational tools, AI-assisted question solving, and related utilities.

This Privacy Policy explains how SmartCalc handles information when you use the App.

2. Information We Collect

SmartCalc does not require you to create an account or provide your name, phone number, email address, or other personal information to use the App.

Information provided when using the AI Solver

If you choose to use the AI Question Solver, the question or other content that you submit may be transmitted to our AI service so that the requested solution can be generated.

If you choose to submit an image or photograph as part of an AI question, that image is transmitted to the AI service for the purpose of processing your request.

Please avoid submitting personal, confidential, or sensitive information in questions or images.

Advertising information

SmartCalc uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as an IP address, advertising or device identifiers, diagnostic information, and information about interactions with advertisements and the application. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related purposes.

For information about Google's handling of data through its advertising services, please refer to Google's privacy information.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed through SmartCalc is used to:

  • Provide AI-generated answers when you request them.
  • Process images submitted to the AI Solver.
  • Display and operate advertisements.
  • Maintain and improve the performance and functionality of the App.
  • Help prevent fraud and abuse associated with advertising services.

SmartCalc does not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

SmartCalc uses third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)

Used to display advertisements in the App. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process certain device, advertising, diagnostic, and interaction information.

AI Processing Service

When you use the AI Solver, the question and, when applicable, the image you submit are transmitted to the AI service used by SmartCalc to generate a response.

Third-party services process information according to their own policies and terms.

5. Camera and Photos

SmartCalc may request camera or photo access when you choose to use image-based question solving.

Camera or photo access is used for the AI Solver functionality. SmartCalc does not access your camera or photos for advertising purposes.

You can choose whether to grant the relevant permission. Features requiring that permission may not function if permission is denied.

6. Data Security

Information transmitted between the App and our services is sent using encrypted connections such as HTTPS where supported.

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through SmartCalc. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

SmartCalc does not require user accounts and therefore does not maintain an account database containing user profiles.

Information submitted to the AI Solver is transmitted for the purpose of processing the requested answer. SmartCalc does not intentionally maintain a personal profile of users based on their AI questions.

Third-party service providers may retain information according to their own privacy policies and retention practices.

8. Children's Privacy

SmartCalc is not intended to collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to use its core calculator functionality.

Parents or guardians should encourage children not to submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information through the AI Solver.

9. Your Choices

You can:

  • Choose whether to grant camera or photo permissions.
  • Choose whether to use the AI Solver.
  • Choose whether to submit an image to the AI Solver.
  • Stop using the App at any time.
  • Use your device's available privacy and advertising controls to manage certain advertising preferences.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when SmartCalc's features, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or SmartCalc's privacy practices, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the SmartCalc Google Play Store listing.
